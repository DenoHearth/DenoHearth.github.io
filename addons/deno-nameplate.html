Deno Nameplate

Clean, compact nameplates for WoW: Forever with health text, cast bars, level and quest icons, full size and font options, and a demon frame with cast bar for your pet.

Download v1.6.1CurseForgeSource on GitHub

Replaces the default nameplates with clean, compact ones: a slim health bar, the unit's name above it, a cast bar below, and small level, elite, raid marker and quest icons around it. Written from scratch for World of Warcraft: Forever and its addon rules.

What it does

  • Friendly and enemy settings are separate

    , each with its own page.

  • Health bar

    Width, height, bar texture, background color.

  • Health text

    In five formats: current, current / max, percent, current / max - percent, or missing health.

  • Cast bar

    With spell icon, and a shield for casts that cannot be interrupted.

  • Level indicator

    Colored by difficulty, a skull for bosses, gold and silver icons for elite and rare-elite units.

  • Raid target icon

    And quest objective icon (left, right or above the name).

  • Colors

    Class colors for players, the game's reaction colors for everything else, grey for units tapped by someone else, red plus a highlight on enemies you have threat on.

  • Your debuffs

    Above enemy nameplates.

  • Crowd control

    (Fear, Polymorph, Sap, stuns ...) as a larger icon over the middle of the enemy plate, with the proc glow and a red number in its last seconds (3 by default, 1 to 10). Yours and every other player's, on every enemy with a visible nameplate.

  • Execute colour

    An enemy bar turns orange under a health percent you set (25 by default).

  • Incoming heals and absorb shields

    Heals being cast show green after the health, shields such as Power Word: Shield show white and striped, with a glow when the shield is bigger than the missing health. Both work in combat.

  • Removable enemy buffs

    Magic buffs that can be dispelled, purged or stolen, in a blue frame beside the plate.

  • Target

    A larger plate (adjustable scale) with a highlight and a bright border.

  • Two styles

    Standard (the compact look, default) or Modern: name above a tall health bar with one-decimal percent, a power bar (mana, energy, rage; it follows druid forms; mobs without power get none) and the cast bar with spell name, time left and the spell icon. Modern can be on enemy plates only (default) or on friendly plates too, and it keeps its own Friendly and Enemy settings, separate from Standard's.

  • Enemy player colour

    With Use Class Color off, enemy players take a colour you pick.

  • Clickable area

    Width and height of the click box, with a toggle that draws the box so you can see it.

  • View distance

    How far away nameplates show, up to the game's maximum of 40 yards.

  • Fonts and textures

    A built-in set; LibSharedMedia entries are added when another addon provides that library. No library is required.

  • Minimap button

    That opens the options; it can be hidden.

  • Demon frame

    (on by default, can be switched off): your pet in the pet frame's own round portrait, with a wider health bar carrying its name and health, its stance above, a thin mana line and a cast bar under it (spell name, icon, time left). Click it to target the pet, right-click for its menu.

  • Ctrl + drag

    Hold Ctrl out of combat and the demon frame and the pet action bar light up blue; drag either one with the left mouse button to move it. The pet bar keeps the game's own buttons, so you change them the usual way (drag spells from the spellbook).

Install

  1. Download the zip from the latest release, or install it from CurseForge.
  2. Extract the DenoNameplate folder into World of Warcraft\<Forever folder>\Interface\AddOns\.
  3. Restart the game.
